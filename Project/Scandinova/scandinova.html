Economy Crisis and Social Change(1920-1940)

Social Democracy Gained influence

  • The industry revolution expanded the labor group, intensifying the conflicts between the classes
  • During 1920s, communist got influences, but lost power soon because of failures in solving economic problems (Glückstadt et al., n.d).
  • Social democracy parts abonded the Marxist ideology about revolution and started to pursue a reformist path, and regained the influence in regional politics. (Bengtsson, 2019)
  • About why socialism did not become the prevalent in scandinavia. One possible explation is that the Nordic countries often conflict with soviet due to geopolitics, that they did not want to become too close to USSR. (For instance, Norwegian Labor party splitted to quit Communist International in 1923)

The Great Depression

  • During 1930s, the great depression spread to Scandinavia, the unemployment rate increased, and the industrial production decreased. For instance, Norway fell by 22 percent (Tvedt,n.d).
  • Three abonded the gold standard to devalue their currency. Sweden's economy condition was improved, while Denmark's and Norway's economic kept worsening. (Stauning et al., n.d.; Tvedt, n.d.)
  • The possible reason is Denmark's economy relied on expression to the UK, while UK adopt conservative police to protect demotic economy, which further worse Denmark's economy.
  • in 1933, Denmark signed the Kanslergade agreement, which allowed the further devaluation of currency. the agreement also restricted the strike action from both the labor and the employer, to stabilize the wages. (Stauning et al., n.d.)
  • While Norway's economy crisis continued until the World war II.

World War II

Three countries abandoned the gold standard to devalue their currency

German Invasion

In 1939, Scandinavia countires declared netural, but German occupy Norway and Denmark. Swenden repeatedly compromise to allow german troops pass through their land

Iceland, which had belonged to Denmark, occupied by Britian. In 1944, Iceland became an indendent public through public vote in 1944

After war reconstruction

German adopted "scorched earth tactcis" before the retreated, burn large area of Norway into ash.

On the other sides, the materials and infrastruction help the reconstruction of Norway

Swenden intoduce a universial social welfare, including free hopital, family support, and pension. Due to the stable trading condition, the economy recovered quickly (Sandvik, 2025).

In 1945, Denmark accept german refugees escape from Soviet, but forbiddened German and Danes to have contact. The refugees was sent back to German in 1949 (Aarhus University.(n.d.))
